| D-022 | 2026-07-30 | The availability engine is one gate. `private.require_bookable_appointment_slot` is the single authority for whether a slot can be claimed, and the slot projection (`api_availability_slots`) and quote (`api_quote_appointment`) answer by calling that same gate per candidate inside a subtransaction rather than reimplementing its rules. | Before P2-07 the Express projection and the RPC each carried their own copy of the rules and had already drifted: the projection could offer a slot that the claim then refused. | D-023 | 2026-07-30 | Chair capacity is enforced as peak concurrency during the candidate's occupied window, serialized by a shop-scoped advisory lock taken before the customer and provider locks. | Counting every appointment that merely overlaps the candidate over-refuses: with two chairs, a 09:30-10:15 and a 10:45-11:30 booking both overlap a 10:00-11:00 candidate but never each other, so the true peak is two, not three. | D-024 | 2026-07-30 | A barber becoming active at a shop is granted the shop's currently active services by default, attributed to the employing owner.
